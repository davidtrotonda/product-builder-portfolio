# TienRank - Ecommerce Rankings and Store Discovery

## Summary

TienRank is a public ecommerce discovery platform for Spanish online stores. It combines category rankings, store profiles, ecommerce news, forum content and AI-readable public routes so people and agents can understand how ecommerce brands are positioned.

## Live project

[Open TienRank](https://tienrank.com/)

## Problem

Ecommerce research is usually fragmented across search results, review sites, social channels and individual store websites. Store owners, agencies and shoppers need a clearer way to discover stores, compare categories and learn from real ecommerce examples.

## What I built

- Public ecommerce rankings.
- Store profile pages.
- Category pages for ecommerce verticals.
- Ecommerce news and editorial content.
- Forum and community surfaces.
- Claim flows for store owners.
- Sitemaps, feeds, structured data and AI-readable routes.
- Public `llms.txt`, `ai-index.json`, `agent-guide.md` and OpenAPI surfaces.

## What I learned

- Programmatic SEO needs trust signals, editorial limits and clear methodology.
- Store profiles are stronger when they separate public facts, editorial interpretation and community signals.
- Rankings can become a distribution loop when stores have a reason to claim, share and link to their profile.
