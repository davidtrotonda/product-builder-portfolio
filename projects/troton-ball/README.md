# Troton Ball — Mobile Game Case Study

## Summary

Troton Ball was another Unity mobile game I built for my blog and early product experiments. It was more polished than Save Troton, but it grew less organically.

## Context

I used Troton Ball to keep learning mobile game development, store publishing and paid acquisition.

## What I built

- Mobile game.
- App store listing.
- Promotional video.
- Paid acquisition test.
- Blog-related product experiment.

## Growth experiment

This project helped me understand that product quality alone is not enough. Distribution, positioning, store presentation and acquisition matter a lot.

## Results

- Around 800 downloads organically.
- Around 1,000 additional downloads with ads.
- Average cost per download around 0.12 EUR.
- No in-app purchases.
- No monetization system inside the game.
- Later removed from Google Play because I did not keep it updated.

## What I learned

- More effort in the product does not guarantee better distribution.
- Store positioning and the hook of the game matter.
- Paid ads need both a good product and a strong acquisition angle.

## What I would improve today

- Improve the first-session experience.
- Add retention and level-completion analytics.
- Test store screenshots and video creatives before spending more on ads.
