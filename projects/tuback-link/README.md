# TuBack.link - Backlink Opportunities for Ecommerce

## Summary

TuBack.link is a backlink opportunity finder for ecommerce stores. It helps founders, SEO teams and online shops discover places where they can create profiles, submit stores, publish useful content or earn relevant mentions by category.

## Live project

[Open TuBack.link](https://tuback.link/)

## Problem

Most ecommerce stores know they need authority, but they do not know where to start without falling into spammy link building. Generic backlink lists are noisy, outdated or not specific enough for ecommerce categories.

## What I built

- Landing page for ecommerce backlink discovery.
- Search experience by ecommerce category and store URL.
- Category pages for backlink opportunities.
- Curated backlink database with source, type, authority estimate and action steps.
- Blog content around ecommerce SEO and link building.
- Public methodology page.
- `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, `ai-index.json` and public API routes.
- Supabase-backed user panel and backlink progress flows.

## What I learned

- Backlink research is more useful when it is tied to category, actionability and fit.
- Nofollow and profile links can still matter as entity signals when they are natural and relevant.
- A curated SEO tool can also become an authority asset when its methodology is public.
